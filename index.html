Live aircraft data: community ADS-B provider (community service; may be unavailable or rate limited). Positions and camera alignment are approximate; horizon calibration and fine adjustment do not guarantee optical accuracy. Recent trails are automatically assembled from positions received while this page is active; it cannot fetch the two minutes before the page opened. Safari may pause updates in the background. Tracks are checked for implausible jumps and are not complete flight histories. Origin/destination come from an unverified community database; a geographic plausibility check blocks clear mismatches, but cannot confirm a route. Automatic route overlays are off by default. Manual override is possible. Route overlay is an illustrative great-circle approximation, not a flight plan or historical track. Relative humidity is a weather-model estimate, not a sensor reading. A model RH ≥90% does not prove an actual condensation trail; contrail persistence depends on ice supersaturation and other conditions. Unmeasured segments remain turquoise, not red. The lower map uses real geographic coordinates; Hungarian county outlines load from an open GeoJSON dataset when online. City coordinates are approximate.
