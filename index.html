Positions and camera alignment are approximate; horizon calibration and fine adjustment do not guarantee optical accuracy. Recent 2-minute trail is automatically assembled from positions received while this page is active; it cannot fetch the two minutes before the page opened. Safari may pause updates in the background. Tracks are checked for implausible jumps and are not complete flight histories. Origin/destination come from an unverified community database; a geographic plausibility check blocks clear mismatches, but cannot confirm a route. Automatic route overlays are off by default.
